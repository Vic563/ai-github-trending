## Editor's Take

Today's dominant story is the "recipe layer" of AI development. Four of the top ten repos frame themselves not as frameworks or platforms but as sets of named, composable skills or workflows you can activate today: book-to-skill, video-shotcraft, obra/superpowers, and img2threejs. This is a notable shift from the infrastructure-first framing of prior weeks, and it suggests the coding-agent ecosystem has crossed a threshold — there are now enough stable base tools that the differentiation has moved to what you do with them. The voice AI comparison cycle that started yesterday is now fully underway: huggingface/speech-to-speech nearly quadrupled its star velocity from yesterday (+227 to +827), while microsoft/VibeVoice held flat at +336 — when two well-resourced open implementations trend simultaneously and one accelerates dramatically, practitioners are actively running both, not just bookmarking. book-to-skill's 3x day-over-day jump from +423 to +1,421 is the chart's most abrupt acceleration and almost certainly reflects a high-reach social post or newsletter feature rather than a code release. The New & Rising section this week tells the clearest structural story: turbo-fieldfare (Gemma 4 in 2GB on Apple Silicon), openclaude-improved (multi-provider coding agent), and agentacct (agent cost tracking) all debut in the same 7-day window, marking July 2026 as the month when inference efficiency, multi-provider routing, and agent observability become their own product category.
