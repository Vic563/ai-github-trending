## Editor's Take

The deepseek-harness acceleration is the defining signal of the week: the project added 17,882 stars on day three, up from 13,212 on day two, making this the first repo to show accelerating velocity across three consecutive days on this chart this year. Yesterday's co-star, cathrynlavery/diagram-design, did not appear in today's trending page or API results at all — its four-day discovery wave ended overnight, a clean contrast that illustrates the difference between a sustained-growth infrastructure story and a viral-content one. The day's most surprising move is ToolJet, which surged from +132 yesterday to +544 today without any apparent announcement, suggesting its "AI agents native to business applications" positioning is finding enterprise practitioners who are past the evaluation phase. A new category appeared today with decionis/agent-safe-pipeline: an authorization layer where agents propose actions but cannot authorize them — the first dedicated agent-authorization architecture to enter this chart, and a signal that production deployments are making "who approves what an agent does" into an infrastructure question rather than a design-time one. On the counter-provenance side, watermarks-remover crossed 10,000 stars (from 9,096 yesterday), continuing at +1,235 today and confirming that AI provenance stripping is a persistent workload rather than a novelty spike.
