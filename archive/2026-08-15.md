## Editor's Take

The harness-infrastructure story deepens today: cordiverse/cordis, the TypeScript plugin framework that powers deepseek-harness, entered GitHub trending for the first time with +616 stars, signaling that developers are pulling at the dependency layer to build DSH-compatible plugins independent of the harness itself. Deepseek-harness climbed from 93,268 to 106,480 stars overnight, confirming the debut momentum has converted into sustained adoption rather than a single-day spike. The most notable shift from yesterday is the simultaneous appearance of two LLM fine-tuning tools on the trending chart: unsloth (+501, continuing) and MakazhanAlpamys/Soup (+303, new debut), the first time two fine-tuning projects have trended together here, a signal that local model training is running as its own independent wave rather than as a subset of the agent-infrastructure story. cathrynlavery/diagram-design posted +3,646 for the fourth consecutive day, essentially flat from yesterday's +3,651, marking the end of the acceleration curve and the beginning of steady-state deployment. A new physical-world frontier opened this week: ShawnPana/phone-harness (let your agent control your phone, eight days old) now sits alongside citrolabs/ego-lite as the second chart entry specifically designed for agents operating beyond the terminal.
