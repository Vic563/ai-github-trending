## Editor's Take

alibaba/open-code-review accelerates to +3,231 today (up from +2,756 yesterday), making it the strongest back-to-back velocity this report has tracked, and the story of the week isn't code review — it's that Tencent placed two repos in the top 4: BrowserSkill (#3, +1,350) lets agents operate your real, logged-in browser session without interrupting your work, and WeKnora (#4, +1,197) remains the most-starred new RAG platform for the third consecutive day. The browser-access pattern is the real news here, filling a gap between computer-use (which takes control) and purely headless approaches. Compared to yesterday, SnailSploit/Claude-Red and multimodal-art-projection/YuE both dropped out of trending, confirming that Monday's security-tooling spike was a single-day event while the agent-infrastructure category keeps compounding. Three new Source 2 entries enter the top 20: XiaoDuoYa/codex-with-chatgpt (#12, using ChatGPT as the planning brain inside a Codex harness), Vincentwei1021/anything2explainer (#14, any topic into a narrated explainer video), and ApodexAI/FrontierAgent (#18, a native-TUI multi-agent framework). The wider signal is consistent: production teams are wiring multi-model, multi-skill pipelines, and the tooling to do that reliably — browser access, memory, hallucination grounding, observability — is pulling more attention than raw new capabilities.
