## Editor's Take

Today's dominant theme is **agent skills as a platform**: three of the top trending repos — tt-a1i/archify (+4,562 stars), K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills (+720), and Anthropic's claude-plugins-official (+457) — are all installable skill packages that extend what coding agents can do, treating composition as a first-class engineering problem rather than a one-off prompt. The creative media wave landed hard alongside them: calesthio/OpenMontage (+1,144) and freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2 (+1,687) reflect serious investment in agentic video and image pipelines, now with agent-readable skill interfaces rather than raw scripts. On the infrastructure side, guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover climbed to 19K stars in 18 days — a sign that AI content provenance and C2PA-compliant watermark detection is emerging as its own category, likely accelerated by EU AI Act compliance timelines. The MCP ecosystem continued compounding: cinderline/northcinder and duty1g/x64dbg-mcp-server both cleared 1K stars within a week of launch, and two brand-new repos (codex-with-chatgpt and sepia) each pulled 500+ stars on day one.
